What the algorithm does

Scalar multiplication means repeated point addition. It does not mean multiplying x and y separately.

Q = kG = G + G + ... + G (k times)

For distinct points P and R, calculate the slope λ, then the resulting coordinates:

λ = (y₂ − y₁) · (x₂ − x₁)⁻¹ mod 17 x₃ = λ² − x₁ − x₂ mod 17 y₃ = λ(x₁ − x₃) − y₁ mod 17

For point doubling, use λ = 3x₁² · (2y₁)⁻¹ mod 17. The inverse is a modular multiplicative inverse.

Interpretation, not overclaiming

  • Scalar multiplication is deterministic and easy to compute forward.
  • The ECDLP is difficult only when the curve and group parameters are cryptographically suitable.
  • On this tiny curve, brute force is trivial and outputs repeat periodically.
  • Uniform-looking frequencies or a Hamming-distance change here do not establish cryptographic security or a hash-like avalanche effect.
