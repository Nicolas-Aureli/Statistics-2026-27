Model notes

A
Bernoulli increments

Xᵢ = 1 with probability p, otherwise 0.

E[Xᵢ] = pVar(Xᵢ) = p(1 − p)
B
Rademacher increments

Xᵢ = +1 or −1 with equal probability.

E[Xᵢ] = 0Var(Xᵢ) = 1
C
Law of Large Numbers

The sample average approaches the expected increment as n grows.

Sₙ/n → E[Xᵢ]
D
Central Limit Theorem

The normalized Rademacher sum approaches a standard normal law.

Sₙ/√n ⇒ N(0, 1)

05 / Interpretation

Run a simulation to generate an interpretation of the results.

What do n and m mean?

n — systems / steps. A larger n increases the scale of the total, while the average becomes more stable.

m — attackers. The original model does not specify how m changes p. The optional relation pₘ = 1 − (1 − q)ᵐ is one transparent assumption to explore the effect of more attackers.

Limitation. Real attacks may not be independent or identically distributed. This model illustrates statistical principles, not real-world attack predictions.
